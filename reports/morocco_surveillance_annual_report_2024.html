Stability

Changes of source table, by year (number of diseases affected): 2017 (9), 2022 (3). Series that cross a change should be compared with care; the dashboard marks each one.

Attributes the yearbooks cannot measure

Sensitivity, predictive value positive and representativeness need case definitions, reporting sources and laboratory confirmation, which the yearbooks do not publish. Simplicity, flexibility and acceptability concern the reporting system itself. A field evaluation with Direction de l'Épidémiologie et de Lutte contre les Maladies (DELM) would be needed for these.

Methods and limitations

Data. Cases notified to the Ministry of Health and printed in the province tables of the Santé en chiffres yearbooks, extracted automatically from the PDFs; each number is traceable to its yearbook, table and page. Provinces created after 2003 are reported within their parent province until the yearbook first prints them separately. Population: censuses 2004, 2014 and 2024, interpolated.

Signals. Farrington algorithm on annual counts: a quasi-Poisson model of each province's previous years (up to 7, same yearbook table unless a disease is configured otherwise, 2020–2021 excluded), with a population offset, past outbreaks down-weighted and a trend kept only when significant; a province signals when its count exceeds the upper 97.5% prediction limit and is at least 5. Validated against R's surveillance package.

Smoothed incidence and clusters. Empirical Bayes smoothing (Marshall 1991); local Moran's I (Anselin 1995) on smoothed rates with queen contiguity, 999 conditional permutations, two-sided p < 0.05, not corrected for multiple testing.

Limitations. Notified cases undercount true incidence and completeness varies by disease, province and year; case definitions are those of the national notifiable-disease system and are not printed in the yearbooks; the yearbooks changed tables in 2017 and print fewer diseases from 2022; numbers are reproduced as printed, including inconsistencies flagged under "Data quality".
